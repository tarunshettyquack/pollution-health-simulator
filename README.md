# Longitudinal Air Pollution & Pharmacological Trade-Off Simulator

An agent-based Monte Carlo simulation in Python that models how chronic and acute exposure to air pollution ($\text{PM}_{2.5}$, $\text{NO}_2$, $\text{O}_3$, $\text{SO}_2$) drives disease, degrades lung health over a human lifespan, and alters life expectancy ($\Delta LE$). It also evaluates how **geographic relocation** (moving to cleaner air) and **pharmacological interventions** (dosing, efficacy, and side-effect toxicity) shift individual and cohort outcomes.

## How It Works
Every simulated person starts at birth with a baseline lung health score (`100.0`) and is stepped year-by-year until death:
1. **Environmental Exposure & Relative Risk:** Excess pollutant concentrations above WHO guidelines scale age-specific incidence probabilities for cardiopulmonary diseases (asthma, COPD, ischemic heart disease, stroke, lung cancer, and lower respiratory infections)[cite: 3, 4].
2. **Reversible vs. Permanent Lung Damage:** Severe pollution degrades both acute lung health and the maximum recoverable lung capacity ceiling. Relocating to a cleaner environment allows reversible airway inflammation to recover asymptotically toward the remaining ceiling.
3. **Pharmacometric Benefit vs. Toxicity:** Drugs reduce disease severity and mortality via saturating $E_{\max}$ dose-response curves, balanced against quadratic dose-dependent side-effect penalties, age sensitivity multipliers, and overdose mortality risk.
4. **Cohort Outputs:** Generates life expectancy estimates, annual lung health trajectories, and summary CSVs and PNG plots inside `output/`.

## Repository Structure

| File / Folder | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| `main.py` | Defines exposure/relocation and drug plans, runs the Monte Carlo cohort, and saves charts/tables |
| `data_sorter.py` | Automated ingestion script that normalizes raw WHO, IHME GBD, and OpenAQ/CPCB CSVs into simulation schemas |
| `data/pollutants.csv` | Pollutant names, units, and WHO baseline guideline concentrations[cite: 3, 4] |
| `data/diseases.csv` | Yearly incidence per age group (`inc_<group>`), severity weights, case fatality, and pollutant relative risks |
| `data/drugs.csv` | Target diseases, saturating $E_{\max}$ effects, standard/max safe doses, side-effect penalties, and overdose risks |
| `data/locations_pm25.csv` | Historical and regional air quality profiles generated by `data_sorter.py` |
| `raw_data/` | Drop raw epidemiological or air-quality CSV files here for automatic sorting |
| `output/` | Stores generated summary CSVs and lung health trajectory plots |
